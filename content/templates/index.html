We don't retain your data, or work with companies who do

Our code is open source and cryptographically attested, so you can verify exactly how your data is handled. We don't track user and billing accounts, and only keep statistics to calculate usage.

No guesswork is involved in sending data to us. Our providers are European owned, host their infrastructure in Europe, and implement GDPR Article 5(1) requirements for data minimisation and confidentiality.
