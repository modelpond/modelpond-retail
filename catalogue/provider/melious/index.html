Provider

Melious

Germany · Text, System One, Speech, Image, Embeddings, Rerankers, Moderation

Melious AI GmbH routes open-weight inference across European partner providers and says requests never leave the EU, with no US or APAC fallback. Its DPA promises zero data retention for Melious-operated servers and the providers marked in its subprocessor annex: inputs and outputs stay in volatile memory and are neither logged nor used for training. Stateful Batch, Files, vector-store and vault content, operational metadata, and unmarked upstream infrastructure sit outside that zero-retention commitment; Melious itself records request IDs, token counts, model and provider, cost and energy figures, but not prompt or response text.
